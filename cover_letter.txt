==========================================================================
SUMMARY
==========================================================================

I derive the cross-coupling between the gravitational sector (Paper II) and the gauge sector (Paper VI) on the DDD substrate. The result is a small but non-zero drift rate beta_R = R_0_dot / R_0 of the substrate's reference scale, which translates into a slow drift of the fine-structure constant alpha_EM_dot / alpha_EM at the level constrained by atomic-clock experiments. The framework therefore predicts a specific relation between cosmological dark-energy evolution and the laboratory bound on alpha drift.

==========================================================================
WHY THIS MATTERS
==========================================================================

Atomic-clock comparisons constrain alpha drift to |alpha_EM_dot / alpha_EM| < 10^-17 / yr. Connecting this bound to cosmological dark-energy evolution is a non-trivial consistency requirement that DDD addresses without new parameters.

==========================================================================
NOVELTY
==========================================================================

Joint Wilson-loop back-reaction calculation. Derivation of beta_R from the cross-coupling coefficient. Comparison with current atomic-clock bounds and with the cosmogenetic-cascade dilution rate of Paper VIII.
